# Mission Framing: CLO3 / Continuity and Applications

## Mission Identity

- target_clo: `CLO3`
- module: `continuity-and-applications`
- mission_family: `sbra-step-based-reasoning`
- bloom: `Analyze`

## Evidence Target

- evidence type: `SBRA candidate`
- assessment role: `formative`
- badge hook: `clo3-analyze-continuity`

## Why This Mission Exists

- Learners should move beyond memorizing the word "continuous" and show that they can test the needed conditions in the right order.
- This mission should diagnose whether the learner can distinguish a valid continuity argument from a graph-based guess.

## Misconceptions Worth Targeting

- deciding continuity only from a smooth-looking sketch without checking the function value and limit
- checking only one continuity condition and assuming the rest automatically hold
- mixing up point continuity with interval continuity
- giving a yes-or-no answer without explaining which condition fails

## Step Intent Draft

1. identify the exact continuity claim and the point or interval being tested
2. check the required continuity conditions in a valid order
3. decide whether each condition is satisfied from the given information
4. close with a continuity judgment that names the supporting reason or failure point
