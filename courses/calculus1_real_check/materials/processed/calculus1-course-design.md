# Calculus 1 Course Design

## 1. Course Direction

- Course code: `SMAC001`
- Course name: `Calculus 1`
- Course style: first-course calculus, concept-to-technique progression, reasoning plus routine practice
- TQF3 source anchor: `tqf3 calculus1.md`

## 2. CLO Summary

| CLO | Bloom | Short Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| CLO1 | Understand | explain the key ideas of limits, continuity, derivatives, and integrals |
| CLO2 | Apply | compute standard calculus results with appropriate techniques |
| CLO3 | Analyze | analyze function behavior using continuity, derivatives, and definite integrals |
| CLO4 | Evaluate | justify process choices and interpret results in context |

### 3.1 Module Structure

| Module | Suggested Weeks | Core Content | Main CLOs |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Limits and Functions | 2 | limit theorem, one-sided limits, infinite limits, graph and function behavior near a point | CLO1, CLO2 |
| Continuity and Applications | 3 | continuity at a point, continuity on an interval, continuity conditions and common failure modes | CLO1, CLO3 |
| Differentiation | 4-10 | derivative definition, derivative rules, trigonometric and exponential/logarithmic derivatives, extrema, graph analysis, related applications, indeterminate forms, L'Hospital | CLO2, CLO3, CLO4 |
| Integration | 11-14 | antiderivatives, indefinite and definite integrals, substitution, Fundamental Theorem of Calculus, integration techniques | CLO1, CLO2, CLO4 |

### 3.2 Week-by-Week Plan

| Week | Theme | Suggested Interactive Materials | Main Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 2 | limit theorem, one-sided limits, infinite limits | graph-reading warm-up, limit comparison cards, quick routine check | limit explanation and short computation check |
| 3 | continuity at a point and on an interval | continuity diagnosis table, graph-sort discussion, condition checklist | continuity decision with justification |
| 4-6 | derivative definition and basic derivative rules | derivative rule map, symbolic practice ladder, pair explanation | derivative routine practice and mini reasoning check |
| 7-9 | applications of derivatives, extrema, graph behavior | optimization storyboard, graph-sign table workshop, application discussion | application analysis and graph interpretation |
| 10 | indeterminate forms and L'Hospital | condition check drill, compare-before-use routine | justified choice of L'Hospital or another method |
| 11 | antiderivatives and indefinite integrals | antiderivative sorting, substitution cue cards | antiderivative and substitution practice |
| 12 | Fundamental Theorem of Calculus and definite integrals | accumulation story, FTC match-and-explain, bound interpretation | FTC explanation and definite-integral reasoning |
| 13-14 | integration techniques | technique chooser, worked-example comparison, peer explanation | integration-technique practice set |
