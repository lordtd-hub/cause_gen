## Course Description Anchor

Calculus 1 covers limits, continuity, derivatives of algebraic and transcendental functions, applications of derivatives, indeterminate forms and L'Hospital's rule, antiderivatives, indefinite and definite integrals, the Fundamental Theorem of Calculus, and introductory integration techniques.
