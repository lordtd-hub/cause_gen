# Continuity and Applications

Use continuity as a bridge between limit ideas and richer function analysis.

## Learning Snapshot

- Learning pace: 3 week(s)
- Main CLO emphasis: CLO1, CLO3
- Module style: application

## What You Will Explore

In this module, learners gradually work through continuity at a point, continuity on an interval, continuity conditions and common failure modes.

The main ideas you will revisit in this part of the course are:

- continuity at a point and on an interval

## Learning Flow

- Week 3: continuity at a point and on an interval

## Try While You Learn

- continuity diagnosis table, graph-sort discussion, condition checklist

## Check Your Understanding

- continuity decision with justification
