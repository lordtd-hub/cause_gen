# Differentiation

Build derivative concepts, rules, and function-analysis techniques for change and rate.

## Learning Snapshot

- Learning pace: 4-10 week(s)
- Main CLO emphasis: CLO2, CLO3, CLO4
- Module style: application

## What You Will Explore

In this module, learners gradually work through derivative definition, derivative rules, trigonometric and exponential/logarithmic derivatives, extrema, graph analysis, related applications, indeterminate forms, L'Hospital.

The main ideas you will revisit in this part of the course are:

- derivative definition and basic derivative rules
- applications of derivatives, extrema, graph behavior
- indeterminate forms and L'Hospital

## Learning Flow

- Week 4-6: derivative definition and basic derivative rules
- Week 7-9: applications of derivatives, extrema, graph behavior
- Week 10: indeterminate forms and L'Hospital

## Try While You Learn

- derivative rule map, symbolic practice ladder, pair explanation
- optimization storyboard, graph-sign table workshop, application discussion
- condition check drill, compare-before-use routine

## Check Your Understanding

- derivative routine practice and mini reasoning check
- application analysis and graph interpretation
- justified choice of L'Hospital or another method
