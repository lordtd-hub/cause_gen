# Integration

Connect area, accumulation, antiderivatives, and the first major links between derivative and integral ideas.

## Learning Snapshot

- Learning pace: 11-14 week(s)
- Main CLO emphasis: CLO1, CLO2, CLO4
- Module style: application

## What You Will Explore

In this module, learners gradually work through antiderivatives, indefinite and definite integrals, substitution, Fundamental Theorem of Calculus, integration techniques.

The main ideas you will revisit in this part of the course are:

- antiderivatives and indefinite integrals
- Fundamental Theorem of Calculus and definite integrals
- integration techniques

## Learning Flow

- Week 11: antiderivatives and indefinite integrals
- Week 12: Fundamental Theorem of Calculus and definite integrals
- Week 13-14: integration techniques

## Try While You Learn

- antiderivative sorting, substitution cue cards
- accumulation story, FTC match-and-explain, bound interpretation
- technique chooser, worked-example comparison, peer explanation

## Check Your Understanding

- antiderivative and substitution practice
- FTC explanation and definite-integral reasoning
- integration-technique practice set
