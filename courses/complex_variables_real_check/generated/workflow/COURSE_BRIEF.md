## CLO Snapshot

- CLO1: Explain the algebraic and geometric foundations of complex numbers. (Understand)
- CLO2: Analyze limits and continuity of complex functions using prior analysis knowledge. (Analyze)
- CLO3: Explain and apply complex differentiation, analytic functions, and holomorphic functions. (Apply)
- CLO4: Explain and compute complex integrals along suitable paths. (Apply)
- CLO5: Use Taylor and Laurent series to study complex functions and support integral reasoning. (Analyze)
