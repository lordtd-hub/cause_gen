# Complex Variables Course Design

## 3.1 Module Structure

| Module | Suggested Weeks | Main CLOs | Core Content |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Complex Number Foundations | 1 | CLO1 | algebraic form, geometric form, modulus, argument, and the complex plane |
| Limits and Continuity in the Complex Plane | 2-3 | CLO2 | limits of sequences and functions, continuity, and transfer from real analysis |
| Complex Differentiation and Analytic Functions | 4-6 | CLO3 | complex derivative meaning, analytic functions, and holomorphic structure |
| Complex Integration | 7-8 | CLO4 | complex line integrals, path meaning, and links to earlier integral ideas |
| Taylor, Laurent, and Applications | 10-16 | CLO5 | power-series reasoning, Laurent series, and applications to complex integrals |

## 3.2 Week-by-Week Plan

| Week | Theme | Suggested Interactive Materials | Main Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | algebraic and geometric language of complex numbers | argand diagram warm-up, representation matching | concept explanation check |
| 2 | sequence, limit, and continuity review for complex values | limit comparison prompts, continuity checkpoints | worked continuity explanation |
| 3 | continuity reasoning in the complex plane | continuity diagnosis cards, example/non-example sorting | reasoning check on continuity |
| 4 | meaning of the complex derivative | derivative comparison prompts, stepwise explanation | derivative setup task |
| 5 | analytic and holomorphic functions | condition-check activity, analytic/non-analytic examples | analytic-condition explanation |
| 6 | derivative reasoning practice | guided derivation sequence, reflection prompt | SBRA candidate on derivative choice |
| 7 | meaning of complex integration | path setup cards, contour examples | contour setup explanation |
| 8 | complex integral practice | worked contour task, short computation sequence | computation and explanation task |
| 10-11 | polynomial, rational, and exponential links to series | series expansion prompts, local behavior comparison | series setup check |
| 12-13 | trigonometric and logarithmic complex functions | representation comparison, branch discussion | reasoning note |
| 14 | Taylor-series perspective | series-choice comparison | expansion explanation |
| 15 | Laurent-series perspective | singularity and local-behavior prompts | local behavior analysis |
| 16 | application of Taylor and Laurent ideas to integrals | application workshop, summary reflection | SBRA candidate on series-based reasoning |
