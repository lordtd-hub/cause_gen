# TQF3 Teaching Method Map

| source_method | activity_family | used_in | note |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| lecture plus discussion | guided discussion | complex-number-foundations | anchor notation and geometric meaning with shared examples |
| lecture plus discussion plus practice | guided exploration | limits-and-continuity | move from real-variable review to complex-plane reasoning |
| lecture plus discussion plus independent study | proof practice | complex-differentiation | connect formal derivative conditions to stepwise reasoning |
| lecture plus discussion | worked examples | complex-integration | model contour setup before asking learners to compute on their own |
| lecture plus discussion plus practice | theorem-condition diagnosis | series-and-applications | compare Taylor and Laurent choices and inspect when each is useful |
