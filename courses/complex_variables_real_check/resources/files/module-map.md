# Module Map

## CLO Overview

- CLO1: Explain the algebraic and geometric foundations of complex numbers.
- CLO2: Analyze limits and continuity of complex functions using prior analysis knowledge.
- CLO3: Explain and apply complex differentiation, analytic functions, and holomorphic functions.
- CLO4: Explain and compute complex integrals along suitable paths.
- CLO5: Use Taylor and Laurent series to study complex functions and support integral reasoning.

## Module Overview

- 01. Complex Number Foundations | kind: concept | CLOs: CLO1
- 02. Limits and Continuity in the Complex Plane | kind: concept | CLOs: CLO2
- 03. Complex Differentiation and Analytic Functions | kind: concept | CLOs: CLO3
- 04. Complex Integration | kind: application | CLOs: CLO4
- 05. Taylor, Laurent, and Applications | kind: application | CLOs: CLO5
