# Module Map

## CLO Overview

- CLO1: Explain core AI concepts, terminology, and problem domains.
- CLO2: Apply search, reasoning, and representation ideas to structured AI tasks.
- CLO3: Analyze AI methods, model behavior, and suitable solution strategies.
- CLO4: Evaluate AI use cases, limitations, and ethical implications in practical settings.

## Module Overview

- 01. AI Foundations | kind: concept | CLOs: CLO1, CLO4
- 02. Search and Problem Solving | kind: application | CLOs: CLO2, CLO3
- 03. Knowledge and Reasoning | kind: application | CLOs: CLO2, CLO3
- 04. Intelligent Systems and Ethics | kind: proof | CLOs: CLO1, CLO4
