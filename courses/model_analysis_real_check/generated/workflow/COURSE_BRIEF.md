## CLO Snapshot

- CLO1: Explain mathematical and statistical techniques used in mathematical modeling. (Understand)
- CLO2: Search and review modeling work connected to real-world problems. (Analyze)
- CLO3: Integrate knowledge from other areas to create more complete mathematical models. (Create)
- CLO4: Build mathematical models that are analyzed, tested, and checked for validity. (Apply)
- CLO5: Apply models to real-world problems and interpret results effectively. (Evaluate)
- CLO6: Demonstrate ethics in the development and analysis of mathematical models. (Evaluate)
