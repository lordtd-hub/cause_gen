# Module Map

## CLO Overview

- CLO1: Explain mathematical and statistical techniques used in mathematical modeling.
- CLO2: Search and review modeling work connected to real-world problems.
- CLO3: Integrate knowledge from other areas to create more complete mathematical models.
- CLO4: Build mathematical models that are analyzed, tested, and checked for validity.
- CLO5: Apply models to real-world problems and interpret results effectively.
- CLO6: Demonstrate ethics in the development and analysis of mathematical models.

## Module Overview

- 01. Modeling Foundations | kind: concept | CLOs: CLO1, CLO2
- 02. Modeling Tools and Techniques | kind: concept | CLOs: CLO1, CLO3
- 03. Linear and Nonlinear Models | kind: application | CLOs: CLO3, CLO5
- 04. Model Development and Probability Models | kind: application | CLOs: CLO3, CLO5
- 05. Validation and Advanced Cases | kind: concept | CLOs: CLO2, CLO4, CLO6
- 06. Project Modeling Studio | kind: proof | CLOs: CLO3, CLO4, CLO5, CLO6
